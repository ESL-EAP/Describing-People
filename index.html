Task 1

First impressions

Talk with a partner. Choose two questions. You may pass on a personal question.

1. When you meet someone for the first time, what do you notice?
Their clothes? Their smile? Their voice? Something else?
2. Can you guess someone’s personality from how they look?
Why or why not?
3. Have you ever had the wrong first impression of someone?
What happened? You can talk about a person in a book, film, or TV show.
4. Can someone look shy but be friendly?
Can you think of another example?
Helpful phrasesI notice ___ first. · I think ___ because ___. · Sometimes, but ___. · I once thought ___, but then ___. · For example, ___. · I agree. / I’m not sure I agree.

Remember: A first impression is a guess. We learn more about a person when we talk to them.

Task 4 · Movie casting team

The Last Call

11:47 p.m.

A journalist receives a voicemail from a person who disappeared ten years ago. The message gives one clue and says:

“Don’t trust the person who finds me first.”

The journalist has until morning to find out who sent the message.

Your job: You are the casting team. Choose actors for five roles from seven candidates. Two actors will not be cast.

The five roles

Investigative JournalistAsks direct questions, talks to many people, and keeps looking when answers are difficult.
ResearcherListens closely, checks information carefully, and notices small details in clues.
Podcast HostSpeaks clearly and confidently, explains information, and keeps listeners interested.
Local ContactWelcomes the journalist, builds trust, knows the area, and offers practical help.
Skeptical WitnessStays calm, asks for proof, and does not believe a story immediately.
Trait word boxfriendly · helpful · rude · impatient · quiet · talkative · confident · patient · careful · curious — or write your own word.

Use different evidence: appearance helps you identify the actor. The actor’s actions help you infer personality. The role description helps you cast the movie.

Candidate gallery

Meet the seven actors

Look at each photo and play the introduction. The actors do not say personality words. Write short notes, not every sentence. You can listen again.

Group decision

Cast the movie

Work with your group. Choose one actor for each role. Explain each choice with an action or example from the audio.
